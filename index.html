Easy Recipes

Here are simple steps to make a waffle:

  1. Mix dry ingredients: flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt into a bowl.
  2. Mix wet ingredients: milk, eggs, butter/oil, and vanilla.
  3. After making two seperate mixtures, mix them together.
  4. Grease the waffle iron with oil or butter before pitting the batter in.
  5. Cook for 3-5 minutes before golden brown.
  6. Serve and enjoy with syrup or toppings of your choice!

Dairy Free Replacements:

  • Instead of cow milk, add water or almond milk
  • Instead of melted butter, add canola oil

FUN FACT: Eggs are entirely dairy free!

Waffles originated from ancient Greek. They were cakes cooked with hot plates, later evolving into iron-pressed wafers. Early recipes used flour, milk, and eggs.

Waffles are a popular breakfast food with a crispy layer. They can be served with a variety of toppings like syrup, fruits, etc. They make for a quick and delicious meal.🧇
